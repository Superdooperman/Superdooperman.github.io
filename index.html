📲 Add any app to your home screen

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the app
  2. Tap Share ⬆️
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the app
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen
Desktop (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open the app
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar
  3. It then launches like a native app, even offline
