# Radioactive Rooster and the Yolk of Midnight

A one-sitting point-and-click episode. Radioactive Rooster crosses Cluck County in a bad disguise to get the Atomic Egg back from The Cock Gobbler before she hard-boils it at the Hot Oil Pavilion. Midnight is the threat. There is no clock to race.

The design notes, puzzle key, crow-down script, and the list of things still needed are in [docs/Radioactive-Rooster-Roadmap.pdf](docs/Radioactive-Rooster-Roadmap.pdf).

## Play

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8770 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8770/

Three save slots live in the browser under `rr-yolk-saves`. Phone taps work. Add `?spots=1` to outline the clickable spots, or toggle them from Menu.

Verb bar: Look at, Talk to, Take, Use, Push. Keys 1 through 5 select those verbs. Space or Enter advances a caption. Esc clears a held item or closes the menu. Exits are buttons. To use an item, click it, then click a spot or another item. Look is the default verb.

```bash
node --test test/puzzle.test.js
```

## The crow

His crow is the rooster call. The Atomic Egg powers it. Without the egg the call is a wheeze, written `*pah*`. The only full **PAH-KAW!!!** is the ending, after the egg is saved. Blurting "I am the crow that curdles crime" is the failed stealth line, not the powered call.

## Mask and feathers

The big white eyes are stuffed onto the suit. Their pupils point in wonky, disagreeing directions, like googly eyes. His real eyes are under the mask. He sees through the nostril holes in the beak.

The red feathers under his arms stay tucked. They snap out when he jumps from a height or glides, then tuck away again. In this episode that happens on the drop down the fire escape. The full-body concept drawing is a glide, so the feathers in that drawing are the right pose for it. Standing, squatting, and crowing should show sleeves, not wings.

## Tone

Silly superhero, good heart, no patience for evildoers. Darkwing Duck, Earthworm Jim, and Inspector Gadget are the mood. Mildly PG-13. No nudity, no sexual acts, no gore. The script does not use anatomical words for the wattle. The double meaning is allowed once in a scene. The fair is a nod to the 1950 film Gun Crazy: he falls for her at first sight, she invites him into the crime, and he refuses because he is too good. Nobody dies, and there are no guns.

Concept drawings in `art/concept/` are signed Mike Azzato, Aug 07.

## Art: pixel and fancy

Pixel art (`art/pixel/`) is the default and is generated by `python3 tools/pixel/build.py`. A high-res set goes in `art/fancy/` with the same file names, with no code changes. Missing files fall back to pixel art, then to the old stand-ins in `art/placeholders/`. Toggle with the Art button or the G key. Scene coordinates are logical 320×180 pixels. See [ART-SPEC.md](ART-SPEC.md).

Music and voices can come later. Until a file exists, cues are a small synth and music beds stay silent. Filenames are listed in `audio/in/README.md`.

Backgrounds are 1280 by 720. The hero is not painted into the rooms. He appears as a portrait, and as a squat or crow overlay.
