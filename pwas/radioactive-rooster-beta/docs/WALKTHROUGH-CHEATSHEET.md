# Radioactive Rooster: winning-route cheat sheet (beta)
This matches the automated "hero" test run (tools: /workspace/pwt-tools/rooster-walkthrough.mjs). Edit either and tell Vegapunk.

Tip: double-click an edge exit to go straight to the next scene. Hints (hover highlights) are off by default; turn them on in the menu.

## 1. Rooftop coop
1. Look at the **bucket**.
2. Take the **coop lamp** (you get the spare bulb; the roof goes dark).
3. Take the **old crates** (geiger counter).
4. Use the **coop door** (photo).
5. Go through the **roof hatch** (down to the fence).

## 2. Fence and henhouse
6. Use the **top rail** (sit on it).
7. Go through the **henhouse door**.
8. Take the **tasting spoon** (C.G. monogram).
9. Take the **torn ticket** (stub).
10. Use the **photo** on the **sitting hen**. (He keeps the photo: he signs her a fresh one. Try it on other people too.)
11. Take the **fat grub**.
12. Leave through the **door back**.

## 3. Barn
13. Take the **path to the barn**.
14. Use the **grub** on **Mortgage the goat** (the goat rams the post and you get the blue ribbon).
15. Take the **road to town**.

## 4. Feed & Seed
16. Use the **spoon** on **Ned** (you get hot sauce, a hand mirror, and the bill).
17. Go out the **street door** to WKUK.

## 5. WKUK radio station
18. Combine the **bulb** with the **geiger counter** (rigged counter).
19. Use the **geiger counter** on the **mail slot**. At the choice, pick **pull** (you get the speaker and the map).
20. Take the **fair road** to the gate.

## 6. Fair gate
21. Use the **turnstile** and pick **pull**.
22. Use the **mirror** on the **gate guard**.
23. Use the **prize-rooster cutout** (pass).
24. Use the **turnstile** again.
   - Shortcut alternative for steps 21 to 24: use the turnstile and pick **follow**.

## 7. Midway
25. Talk to the **Jingle Kids** twice.
26. Take **Corny's cash box** (the other ticket half).
27. Combine the **half** with the **stub** (the full ticket).
28. Talk to the **club doorway**. At the choice, pick **pull**.
29. Use the **speaker** on the **ring-toss pegs** (rubber basket).
30. Use the **ribbon** on the **way to the Hot Oil Pavilion** (the rope opens).

## 8. Hot Oil Pavilion (finale)
31. Talk to **Pip**.
32. Talk to **The Cock Gobbler**. Pick **henny**, then **ticket**, then **warn**.
33. Use the **mirror** on the **heat lamp**. (The cage swings out over the oil after the crow-down and jogs down here.)
34. Use the **basket** on the **egg cage**, and you get the Hero ending (the black-and-white front page).

## Alternate route: rigged ring toss, repaying Pip
- At step 28, pick **follow** instead of pull, and skip step 29.
- In the Pavilion: talk to Pip, use **Corny's key ring**, talk to the Gobbler (henny, ticket, warn), use the mirror on the heat lamp, then use the basket on the egg cage.
- Then go out the **tent flap**, use the **tickets** on the Jingle Kids, go back through the rope, talk to Pip, and use the basket on the egg cage again.
