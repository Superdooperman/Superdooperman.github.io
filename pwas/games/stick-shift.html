Stick Shift Simulator

Manual transmission trainer

A browser trainer for a 6-speed H-pattern manual. It models clutch bite, stalling, synchro grind, rev-matching, engine braking, hills, and a scored free-drive.

▶ PLAY

🎮 Controls

Clutch
Ctrl / C / Space, or drag the left pedal / mouse-wheel
Throttle
W or Up, or drag the right pedal
Brake
S or Down
Gears
1–6, N or `, reverse Q+R, or drag the knob
Start
Enter or START (clutch must be down)
