🎮 Controls

Verbs
Look at, Talk to, Take, Use, Push. Keys 1 through 5 select them. Look is the default.
Play
Space or Enter advances a caption. Esc clears a held item or closes the menu. To use an item, tap it, then tap a spot or another item.
Spots
Add ?spots=1 to the address, or toggle spots from the menu.
