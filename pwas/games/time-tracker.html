Rooster Timer

Rooster Timer · Pomodoro + time tracker

A Pomodoro egg timer starring Radioactive Rooster: an egg in his goofy mask with googly eyes that wander, wobbling and cracking as time runs low until it crows PAH KAW! Breaks show him gliding; long breaks are nap time. Focus sessions log to your task tracker with history, stats and export/import. Works offline.

🎮 Controls

Keyboard
Space start/pause, S skip, R reset, M mute.
Phone
Add to home screen; long-press the icon for Start Focus.
