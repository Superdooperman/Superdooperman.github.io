Phish Desk

Trust no inbox

A Papers, Please-style security desk. Triage flagged emails on the computer and texts on the phone: stamp PHISH, LEGIT, or ESCALATE using headers, links, and the handbook. Write company policy each night, resist LAMPREY's bribes, and keep the breach meter down across five days and eleven endings. Early demo with placeholder art; works offline once loaded.

▶ PLAY

🎮 Controls

Desktop
Click the computer or phone on your desk. Hover links to see where they really go, open HEADERS, then click a stamp. Mouse wheel scrolls long messages. In the epilogue, click or Space to advance, Esc to skip.
Phone / tablet
Tap everything; landscape is best. Add to home screen to play it as its own app.
