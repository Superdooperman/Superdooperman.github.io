Video Toolbox

Your clips, edited in the browser

Trim, compress, convert (MP4, WebM, GIF), pull the audio out, join clips and burn in a caption. It runs ffmpeg right in your browser, so nothing is uploaded. The first run downloads the ~32 MB engine once, then it works offline. For files you own or have rights to; it doesn't download from websites.

🎬 How to

Start
Drop a clip (or several, for Join) on the page, pick a tool, press RUN, then SAVE.
Trim
Set start/end from the playhead; frame-step buttons for precision. Fast mode copies without re-encoding.
